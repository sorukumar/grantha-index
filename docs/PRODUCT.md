# Product Brief

**Audience**: Students, builders, and later for IITK / Ministry conversations.

**v1 Goals**:
- List of great Indian books
- Scholarly date ranges
- Digital-readiness indicators
- Shortlist flag

**Non-goals for v1**:
- Reading the books
- Chatbots
- Complex infographics

**Dating Policy**: Ranges + confidence (windows of composition, not dates of revelation).

**Scope**: Canons and famous works, not every manuscript.

**Edge we want**: A union catalog + license + machine-readiness in one place.
